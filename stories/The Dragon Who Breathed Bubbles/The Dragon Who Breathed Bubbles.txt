The Dragon Who Breathed Bubbles

Have you ever wondered what it would feel like to touch a dragon's breath... only to find it wasn't hot at all? Deep in the Obsidian Crags... where the mountains reach up like jagged teeth to bite the sky... lived the Great Fire Drakes. Everything in the Crags was hot. The ground hummed with the heat of hidden lava—thrum... thrum... thrum. The air always smelled of woodsmoke and old iron. The dragons there were proud of their fire. They spent their days practicing their roars and seeing who could melt the largest boulder into a puddle of glowing glass.

But among the giants lived a dragon named Ember. Ember was different. He was smaller than the others... and his scales were not the color of embers at all. They were iridescent... shifting from sea-foam green to a soft... pearly lavender as he moved. And while the other dragons practiced their scorching blasts... Ember had a secret.

One afternoon... high on a ledge overlooking the Smokey Vale... Ember took a deep breath. He felt the air fill his chest... and he prepared to let out a mighty huff. But instead of a roar... and instead of a flame... out came a single... perfect... shimmering bubble.

It was a beautiful thing. It caught the sunlight... spinning and swirling with colors that didn't exist anywhere else in the Crags. It floated on the warm updrafts... bobbing gently—up... and down... up... and down.

Pop.

The bubble burst against a sharp rock... leaving behind a tiny puff of lavender-scented mist. Ember sighed. He wanted to be fierce. He wanted to make the mountains shake with his heat. But no matter how hard he scrunched his eyes or how much he thought about hot soup and summer sun... only bubbles would come out.

The King of the Crags... a massive dragon named Ignis whose scales were as dark as cooled charcoal... noticed Ember’s silence. He landed on Ember’s ledge with a heavy... bone-shaking thud. The ground beneath Ember’s claws trembled.

Ember... Ignis rumbled. His voice sounded like two boulders grinding together. Why is your forge cold? Every dragon must contribute to the heat of the kingdom. Tonight is the Great Glow... when we must light the sky so the travelers can see the mountain paths. Show me your fire.

Ember felt his heart race—thump... thump... thump. He swallowed hard. He didn't want to show the King his secret. But he had no choice. He took the biggest breath he had ever taken... his chest puffed out like a sail... and he blew with all his might.

Thousands of bubbles exploded from his mouth. They were tiny... they were huge... they were shaped like hearts and stars. They filled the air... floating around Ignis’s massive... scowling face. The Crags were suddenly filled with the sound of a thousand tiny bells—plink... plop... tink.

Ignis stared. A medium-sized bubble landed on the tip of his snout... wobbled for a second... and then popped with a soft... cool spray.

Bubbles? Ignis whispered. A dragon who breathes... soap?

The other dragons began to land on the nearby peaks. They let out puffs of smoky laughter. They called Ember the Cloud-Maker. They told him he should go live with the butterflies or the sheep in the valley. Ember felt a deep... heavy sadness in his chest. He folded his wings tight against his back and looked at his claws. He felt very... very small.

But that evening... the weather began to change. The heat that the dragons loved so much became too much. A Great Sultriness settled over the mountains. The air stopped moving. The lava in the cracks began to bubble and hiss... sending up thick... choking clouds of yellow sulfur. The dragons began to pant. Their scales grew dry and itchy. Their tempers grew as hot as their fire.

I am too hot! a young dragon cried... accidentally singeing the tail of his neighbor.

Watch your fire! the neighbor roared... letting out a blast that turned a nearby tree into a pillar of ash.

Soon... the Obsidian Crags were in chaos. The dragons were so grumpy and so overheated that they began to bicker and fight. The sky turned a murky... sickly grey. The travelers on the mountain paths were lost in the thick smoke. The King tried to roar for order... but his throat was so dry that only a raspy cough came out.

Ember watched from his lonely ledge. He saw his friends suffering. He saw the beautiful mountains turning into a place of anger and soot. He knew he had to do something.

He didn't try to be fierce this time. He didn't try to growl. He simply thought about the coolest... freshest thing he could imagine. He thought about the spray of a waterfall... and the feeling of a cold pebble in a mountain stream.

He began to breathe.

He blew a stream of bubbles so thick it looked like a river of light. But these bubbles were different. They were filled with the essence of the morning mist. As they drifted through the sulfur clouds... they began to work. Each bubble acted like a tiny... floating magnet. They pulled the soot and the ash out of the air... trapping the grime inside their shimmering walls.

Pop... pop... pop.

Every time a bubble burst... it released a tiny... cool spray of lavender and mint. The air began to clear. The yellow smoke vanished.

Ember flew into the center of the Crags... his iridescent scales flashing like a beacon. He spun in circles... trailing bubbles behind him like the tail of a comet. He flew over the grumpiest dragons... dusting them with his cool... refreshing mist.

One by one... the dragons stopped fighting. They closed their eyes and lifted their snouts... enjoying the feeling of the moisture on their parched scales. The itching stopped. The tempers cooled. The heavy... hot weight of the afternoon began to lift.

Ember didn't stop. He flew to the Great Lava Pool and blew a massive... giant bubble—the size of a cottage. It hovered over the glowing magma... acting like a giant... translucent shield. The heat stayed down... and the air above grew sweet and breathable once more.

By the time the moon rose over the peaks... the Obsidian Crags were transformed. The air was crystal clear... smelling of damp stone and wild herbs. The dragons were sitting together... not fighting... but talking in low... peaceful tones.

King Ignis walked up to Ember. He didn't roar this time. He leaned down and gently nudged Ember’s shoulder with his nose.

The sky is clear... Ignis said softly. And my throat no longer feels like it is full of dry sand.

I'm sorry I don't have fire... Ember whispered.

Ignis looked up at the moon... then back at the small... iridescent dragon.

Fire can build things... and it can destroy things... the King said. But gentleness... gentleness can heal things. We have enough fire in these mountains... Ember. But we didn't have enough peace. You are the Breath of the Mountain.

From that day on... Ember was no longer called the Cloud-Maker. He was the Caretaker of the Crags. Whenever the heat grew too intense... or the dragons grew too restless... Ember would take a deep breath and fill the sky with his shimmering... lavender-scented wonders.

He realized that you don't have to be loud to be heard... and you don't have to be fierce to be powerful. Sometimes... the most important thing you can bring to a world full of fire... is a little bit of cool... quiet... grace.

As the stars began to twinkle over the clean... dark peaks... Ember stretched out on his ledge. He felt the cool breeze ruffling his wings. He listened to the quiet... steady rhythm of the mountain—thrum... thrum... thrum. He felt a deep sense of belonging... a warmth that had nothing to do with fire... and everything to do with being exactly who he was meant to be.

The world was quiet... the air was sweet... and Ember felt a wonderful... peaceful glow spread through his scales as he watched the bubbles drift toward the stars.
